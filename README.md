# WAB Wittgenstein Chatbot — Comparative Interactions

## From Semantic Search to a Dedicated Wittgenstein Chatbot: Generative AI as Controlled Research Infrastructure

**Filippo Mosca and Alois Pichler**

This page documents the comparative interactions discussed in the article *From Semantic Search to a Dedicated Wittgenstein Chatbot: Generative AI as Controlled Research Infrastructure*.

Five prompts concerning intelligence and artificial intelligence in Wittgenstein were submitted to widely used general-purpose conversational AI systems and to the WAB Wittgenstein Chatbot. All interactions were conducted on 22 July 2026.

For each prompt, the complete interaction includes the user prompt, the response generated by the general-purpose conversational AI system, the response generated by the WAB Wittgenstein Chatbot, and the primary sources retrieved and displayed by the WAB system.

## Technical information

Each interaction records the system/model information and date of interaction alongside the relevant outputs. For the WAB Wittgenstein Chatbot, the records also report the response-generation model, query-processing model, embedding model, search service, and the primary sources retrieved and displayed.
